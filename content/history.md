## Major Milestones

- 2003: [WizEdit](http://code.google.com/p/wizedit/) (now retired), a fully extensible programmers' editor developed for own use.
- 2004: [WizCrypt](http://code.google.com/p/wizcrypt/) (now in maintenance) was one of the first serious attempt at OpenSource development. This was a encryption tool developed for protecting @subwiz's _secrets_.
- 2007: 1.0 release of [RESTClient](https://github.com/wiztools/rest-client). Developed for testing RESTful WebServices at a failed startup by @subwiz and late Ravi Subramaniam.
- 2013: Two books published.
- 2014: Commercial offerings of products made available. [StaGen](https://github.com/wiztools/stagen), the static site generator is released. WizTools.org adopted [StaGen](https://github.com/wiztools/stagen) for its home page.

WizTools.org was inspired by the success of [Apache.org](http://www.apache.org/). @subwiz started contributing to OpenSource projects around 2003, and the domain was registered couple of years later.
